## 👋 About me

I'm a Telecommunications Engineering student at **ETSIT UPM** in Madrid, working where solid backend engineering meets applied AI. For me the hard part of engineering today isn't calling an AI API: it's designing the logic, algorithms and systems that make models useful in the real world.

- 🔭 Building **GRPO Studio** at **ArangoDB** (international scholarship, Arango–UPM chair) and doing **LLM research** at the **GING** group, ETSIT UPM.
- 🧪 Research topics: psycholinguistics in LLMs across **15 languages**, fine-tuning, **GRPO** training and GPU efficiency.
- 🎓 Awarded the **UPM Collaboration Scholarship** (Feb–Jun 2026) and **15 Matrículas de Honor** (top-grade distinctions) so far.
- 🏆 Hackathons: **1st prize** at the Cabify Product Hackathon, plus HackSpain and IndesIA Hack.
- 🌱 Into reinforcement-learning fine-tuning, LoRA, quantization, MCP tools and AI agents that keep a human in the loop.
- 🗣️ Spanish (native) · English (B2)
- 💬 Curious about something? Ask **JavIA**, the AI assistant on [my website](https://javimoralesg.com). It knows my whole background.
- 📫 Open to internships, collaborations and job offers: [contact@javimoralesg.com](mailto:contact@javimoralesg.com)
